Why I Hold Bitcoin Long Term

Personal view · Not financial advice

Personal view, not financial advice. This post is my own opinion and part of my learning journey. I'm not a financial professional. Please do your own research.

When I first started learning about bitcoin, the price moves were the loudest thing about it. Some days it was up a lot, other days down a lot. It was tempting to try to guess the next move. Over time, though, I decided that trying to trade it wasn't for me. Here's why I think about bitcoin as a long-term holding instead.

1. It has a fixed supply

Bitcoin's rules cap the total supply at 21 million coins, and new coins are released on a predictable schedule that slows down over time. Nobody can decide to print more. For me, that's a big part of the appeal: it's a form of money with rules that are public and hard to change.

2. Short-term moves are noise to me

Bitcoin is known for being volatile. I can't predict what it will do next week, and I've accepted that. By thinking in years instead of days, I spend less energy reacting to every headline and more energy learning.

3. It's open to anyone

You don't need permission from a bank to use bitcoin. Anyone with an internet connection can hold it and send it. That idea of open access is exactly why I named this blog "BTC For All."

4. Holding helps me avoid emotional decisions

When I've been tempted to buy because of excitement or sell because of fear, those feelings usually didn't help. Having a long-term plan gives me a simple rule to fall back on.

What I keep in mind

  • Bitcoin can drop significantly, and there's no guarantee it goes up.
  • I only put in money I can afford to leave alone for a long time.
  • I keep learning about security, like how wallets and private keys work.

This approach works for my situation and comfort level. Yours might be different, and that's okay.
