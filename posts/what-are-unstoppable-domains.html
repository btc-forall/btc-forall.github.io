What Are Unstoppable Domains?

Personal view · Not financial advice

Personal view, not financial advice. This post is my own opinion and part of my learning journey. I'm not a financial professional. Please do your own research.

If you've ever sent crypto, you know wallet addresses are long strings of random-looking letters and numbers. One typo and your money could go to the wrong place. Unstoppable Domains are one way to make that friendlier.

The short version

An Unstoppable Domain is a human-readable name, like btcforall.crypto, that's stored on a blockchain. You can link your wallet addresses to it, so people can send you crypto by typing the name instead of a long address.

How is it different from a normal website domain?

  • Ownership: the domain is held in your own crypto wallet, kind of like an NFT, instead of being rented from a registrar.
  • Renewals: many of these domains are bought once rather than renewed every year.
  • Use case: their main job is acting as a simple, readable name for your crypto addresses.

How does sending to one work?

A wallet that supports Unstoppable Domains looks up the name, finds the address linked to it for the coin you're sending, and fills it in for you. Wallets like Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, Brave Wallet and Cake Wallet support this.

Why I use one

It's simply easier to share. Telling someone "send it to btcforall.crypto" is a lot less error-prone than reading out a long address. It's also a fun way to learn hands-on about how blockchain-based names work.

Things to keep in mind

  • Not every wallet or exchange supports these domains, so check before you send.
  • Always confirm the address your wallet resolves before hitting send.
  • Whoever controls the wallet holding the domain controls where it points, so keep that wallet secure.
