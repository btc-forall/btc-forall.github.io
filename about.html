About me

Hi! I'm just a regular person who got curious about bitcoin and decided to actually learn how it works instead of only reading headlines.

I don't work in finance and I'm not a developer. Like a lot of people, I first heard about bitcoin, ignored it, got confused by it, and eventually sat down to understand it properly. Along the way I also started learning about long-term investing in general: saving consistently, thinking in years instead of days, and not panicking when markets get noisy.

Why "BTC For All"?

I believe bitcoin is for everyone. You don't need to be wealthy, technical, or an expert to understand the basics and decide for yourself whether it belongs in your life. This blog is my attempt to make those basics approachable.

What you'll find here

  • Plain-English explanations of bitcoin and crypto concepts
  • Notes on simple, long-term investing habits
  • Honest reflections on what I'm learning, including mistakes
